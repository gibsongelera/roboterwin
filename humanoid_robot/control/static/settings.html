Servo calibration

Put the robot on a stand with its feet off the table before testing servos. Adjust Trim until each joint is straight in the home pose. Use Test to check the direction: HIP + swings the leg forward, KNEE + bends the knee, ANKLE + tilts the body to the robot's right, HEAD + turns left, ARM + raises the arm. If a joint moves the wrong way, tick Invert.
